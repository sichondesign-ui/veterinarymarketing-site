Our history

Born at a kitchen table in Fort Lauderdale

Veterinary Marketing was born with a bold vision at a kitchen table in Fort Lauderdale, where founder John Donato turned passion into purpose. What started as a one-man consulting mission has grown into a full-service veterinary marketing agency that delivers guaranteed results.

In an industry full of outdated tactics and one-size-fits-all solutions, Veterinary Marketing stands apart, driven by data, powered by personalized strategy, and built to elevate veterinary practices to the next level. We've come a long way from that kitchen table, and we're just getting started.

Our office

Less like an office, more like a launchpad

While this is a remote position that gives you the freedom to work where you feel most alive, if you ever want to drop by the office, you'll find more than just exposed ductwork, reclaimed wood, and a beer fridge. Nestled in an Oakland Park neighborhood, our space is open, inspiring, and designed to fuel creativity, connection, and growth.

This isn't some cubicle farm. It's a place where energy flows, ideas spark, and people push each other to be their absolute best. We built it to feel less like an office and more like a launchpad for greatness.

Location, location, location

Minutes from Las Olas

Our office is minutes away from the thriving bars, restaurants, and coffee shops of Las Olas. Our team loves to walk to try one of the many lunchtime vendors, or to work remotely from the comfort of home. After work, it's not uncommon to find us playing pool at our favorite dive bar.
